Our Story

Good vegetarian food, cooked with care, in a place that feels like home.

Where It All Began

Kurry Patta started with a simple idea — bring together the food we grew up loving, soft idlis, crisp dosas, comforting North Indian curries, spicy chaats, all under one roof, made properly, every single day. What began as one small kitchen in Kolkata has grown, but the idea behind it hasn't changed one bit.

Staying 100% Vegetarian

We chose to stay purely vegetarian from day one. Not as a trend, but because it lets us focus — on sourcing well, cooking thoughtfully, and doing justice to every dish on the menu instead of spreading ourselves thin across everything else.

One Table at a Time

From our first outlet, Kurry Patta has grown to two locations across Kolkata — Hungerford Street and Lake Road — while keeping the same kitchen standards and the same warm welcome our guests have come to expect from us.

Food & Hospitality, Our Way

We keep things simple: fresh ingredients, consistent recipes, and people who genuinely care about the guests walking through our doors. Whether you're here for a quick breakfast or a family celebration, we just want you to leave full and happy.
