Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and how to reach us with any privacy questions.

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kurry Patta ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website or get in touch with us through it. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you choose to share with us, mainly through our Contact and Catering enquiry forms. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional, where provided)
  • Your message, event details, guest count or preferred date (for catering enquiries)

We do not collect payment information, passwords, or any sensitive personal data through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

When you submit a form on our website, the details are sent as a pre-filled message to our WhatsApp Business number. We use this information only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry, reservation request or catering request
  • Coordinate event details for catering bookings
  • Improve our service to you

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our Contact and Catering forms open WhatsApp with your details pre-filled so you can send the message directly to us. This means your enquiry is transmitted and stored through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your data. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp's privacy policy for details on how they process your information.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses a small number of third-party services to function properly:

  • Google Maps — embedded on our Locations section to show our outlets and provide directions.
  • Google Fonts — used to load the typefaces on this website.
  • WhatsApp — used to receive messages submitted through our forms.

These services may collect limited technical information (such as your IP address) as part of their standard operation, governed by their own respective privacy policies.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use cookies to track or profile visitors. Some embedded third-party content (like Google Maps) may set its own cookies as part of its normal functioning, which is outside our control.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to your request or fulfil a booking, after which they are not actively used or stored by us beyond your WhatsApp conversation history.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it, at any time by contacting us using the details below. Since most communication happens over WhatsApp, you're also free to delete that conversation from your own device whenever you'd like.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

10. Contact Us
